Shipping and Delivery Policy

Last updated 15 October 2026

NAMO (1namo.com) is operated by AK International, C-143 Ground Floor, Brij Vihar, Ghaziabad, Uttar Pradesh 201011, India (“NAMO”, “we”, “us”).

Where we deliver

Across India, to every pincode our courier partners serve. Your pincode is checked at checkout; an address no courier serves cannot be ordered to.

Cost

Delivery is free. Cash on delivery, where offered, carries a fee of 2% of the order, shown before you place it, and is available for orders up to ₹10,000.

How long it takes

Orders are delivered in 3–7 days. Every product is shipped by NAMO through courier partners (Shiprocket), including products listed by sellers on the Market. You can follow your order in the app once it is dispatched.

Cancelling and returns

You can cancel within 24 hours of ordering, before dispatch, for a full refund. After dispatch, only a damaged, defective or wrong product can be returned, within 7 days of delivery with an unboxing video. See the Cancellation and Refund Policy.

Digital purchases

Downloads, courses and online events are not shipped. They open in the app as soon as they are paid; an event's joining link shows on its page once you have booked.
