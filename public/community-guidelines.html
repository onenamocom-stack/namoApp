Community Guidelines

Last updated 19 October 2026

NAMO is a place for devotion, learning and honest advice. These rules apply to everything you post, comment, message, sell or say on a call. Breaking them can get the content removed and the account blocked.

Not allowed

  • Nudity, sexual content, or sexual messages to anybody.
  • Violence, threats, or content that encourages harm to yourself or others.
  • Hate or abuse against any religion, caste, community, gender or person — including other faiths.
  • Harassment, bullying, stalking, or repeated unwanted messages.
  • Content involving minors in any harmful or sexual way. We report it to the authorities.
  • Spam, fake accounts, impersonating someone, or misleading links.
  • Scams: asking for money outside NAMO, fake remedies or guarantees, “100% sure” predictions.
  • Medical, legal or financial instructions presented as certain. Astrology on NAMO is guidance, not a substitute for a doctor, lawyer or adviser.
  • Selling anything illegal, counterfeit, or not what the listing says.
  • Posting other people’s private information, or content you do not have the right to share.

Report and block

Every post, reel and comment has a menu with Report. A person’s profile and a chat have Report and Block; blocking hides their posts, comments and messages from you. Our team reads every report and removes content or blocks accounts that break these rules, usually within 24 hours. Videos are also checked automatically before they appear.

Astrologers and sellers

Pros and sellers are approved by NAMO and must follow these rules on calls, chats and listings. Never share your phone number to take a consultation or payment outside NAMO.
