Cancellation and Refund Policy

Last updated 30 August 2026

This policy applies to consultation bookings made on Namo (1namo.com), operated by AK International, Ghaziabad, Uttar Pradesh, and paid from your wallet.

When you are charged

Your wallet is debited when a booking is confirmed, not when the session starts. There is no separate hold. This is shown on the booking screen before you confirm.

Cancellation

A confirmed booking cannot be cancelled back by the seeker, and the amount is not returned if you choose not to attend. Book only a slot you intend to keep.

When money comes back

A reversing credit to your wallet, in full, is made in each of these cases:

What happenedWhat we do
The consultant declines your requestFull reversing credit. Nothing was delivered.
The consultant does not turn upFull reversing credit.
A platform failure — no room, no call, our faultFull reversing credit.
You do not attend a session you bookedNothing. This is the policy above.
You ask to cancel beforehandNothing self-service. Our support team may still make a reversing entry at its discretion.

These reversals are not discretionary refunds — they are the price of a service that was not delivered. A “no refund” term does not apply to a session that did not happen.

How reversing credits are paid

A reversing credit is returned to your Namo wallet, normally within minutes and always within 24 hours of the triggering event. From the wallet it can be spent on another booking. Withdrawal of a wallet balance to your original payment method, where offered, is processed within 5–7 business days.

Digital reports and shop items

A digital report is non-refundable once generated. A physical shop item may be returned only if it arrives damaged or wrong; contact us within 48 hours of delivery.

Contact

Raise any refund question at the address on the contact page.
