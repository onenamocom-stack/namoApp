Cancellation and Refund Policy

Last updated 15 October 2026

NAMO (1namo.com) is operated by AK International, C-143 Ground Floor, Brij Vihar, Ghaziabad, Uttar Pradesh 201011, India (“NAMO”, “we”, “us”).

This policy covers everything bought on NAMO. Refunds go back to the way you paid: to your bank or card within 5–7 working days for an online payment, and to your NAMO wallet for anything paid from the wallet.

Products

What happenedWhat we do
You cancel within 24 hours of ordering, before it is dispatchedFull refund.
It has been dispatchedIt can no longer be cancelled; a return is possible only as below.
It arrives damaged, defective or wrongTell us within 7 days of delivery with an unboxing video showing the parcel opened and the fault. We arrange a replacement or a full refund.
You changed your mind after dispatchNo return or refund.
A cash-on-delivery parcel is refused or comes backThe order is cancelled; nothing is owed.

Downloads and courses

A download or a course is yours the moment it is paid, so it is not refundable — except when the file or the lessons do not open for you and we cannot fix it, in which case you get a full refund. Write to us within 7 days.

Events

What happenedWhat we do
The seller cancels the eventFull refund.
You cancel within 24 hours of bookingFull refund.
You cancel later, or do not attendNo refund.

Consultations (paid from the wallet)

Your wallet is debited when a booking is confirmed. A confirmed booking cannot be cancelled by you, and the amount is not returned if you do not attend. A full credit back to your wallet is made when the consultant declines, does not turn up, or the session fails on our side. Wallet credits are normally made within minutes and always within 24 hours.

Wallet balance

Withdrawal of a wallet balance to your original payment method, where offered, is processed within 5–7 working days.

How to ask

Write to support@1namo.com or call +91 99538 08908 with your registered mobile number and the order or transaction reference (and the video for a damaged or wrong product). See the contact page.
