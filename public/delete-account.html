Delete your NAMO account

Last updated 19 October 2026

In the app

  1. Open NAMO and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile → the ☰ menu (Profile settings).
  3. Scroll to the end and tap Delete my account.
  4. Type DELETE and confirm.

Your account is deleted at once. You can do the same on the website at 1namo.com/profile/settings.

Without the app

Write to support@1namo.com from any email, with the mobile number of the account, and ask for it to be deleted. We confirm with a code sent to that number and delete it within 7 days.

What is deleted

  • Your name, mobile number, email, birth details, gender, photo, username, bio and interests.
  • Your login, so the number can no longer sign in to the old account.
  • Your posts, reels, comments and profile page — no longer shown to anybody.
  • Your notifications. A Pro or seller account is closed and your referral code stops working.

What is kept, and why

Records of payments, orders, wallet transactions and earnings are kept without your name for up to 8 years, because Indian tax and accounting law requires it. Money left in your wallet cannot be returned after deletion.
