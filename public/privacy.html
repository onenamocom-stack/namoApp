Privacy Policy

Last updated 30 August 2026

This policy covers Namo, an astrology marketplace operated by AK International (Ghaziabad, Uttar Pradesh, India) at 1namo.com. It explains what we collect, why, and who we share it with.

What we collect

  • Your mobile number — to create your account and sign you in.
  • Your name and email address — that you enter during onboarding.
  • Your birth date, time and place — needed to compute your chart and for a consultant to read for you. Birth place is stored as a location and time zone.
  • Wallet and transaction records — top-ups, bookings and purchases.
  • Booking details — shared with the consultant you book, including the birth details a reading requires.

We do not store card or bank details. Those are handled by Razorpay.

Why we hold it

To run your account, compute charts and horoscopes, let consultants deliver sessions, process payments, and meet legal and tax obligations. We do not sell your data and do not use it for third-party advertising.

Who we share it with

  • Razorpay — to take payments and credit your wallet.
  • Twilio — to send the one-time code that signs you in.
  • Supabase — our database and authentication host.
  • The consultant you book — your name and the birth details for that booking, and nothing else.
  • Authorities — where the law requires it.

Retention

We keep account and transaction records while your account is open and for as long afterwards as tax and accounting law requires. You can ask us to delete your account; records we are legally required to keep are retained for that period only.

Your choices

You can view and correct your name and birth details in the app. To request a copy of your data or its deletion, contact us at the address on the contact page.

Security

Data is stored with row-level access control so one account cannot read another’s. Payment secrets and service keys are never exposed to the app.

Changes

The version on this page is the current one. Material changes will be noted in the app.
