Privacy Policy

Last updated 23 October 2026

NAMO (1namo.com) is operated by AK International, C-143 Ground Floor, Brij Vihar, Ghaziabad, Uttar Pradesh 201011, India (“NAMO”, “we”, “us”).

This policy explains what we collect, why, and who we share it with. It covers the website and the NAMO Android app.

NAMO is for people aged 18 and over.

What we collect

  • Your mobile number, to create your account and sign you in.
  • Your name, email, interests and profile picture, that you give us.
  • Your birth date, time and place, when you use charts or book a consultant.
  • What you post, like, comment, share and message on NAMO, including your chats with consultants and your questions to NAMO AI.
  • Your camera and microphone, only during an audio or video call you start or answer. Calls are not recorded.
  • A notification token for your phone or browser, so we can tell you about calls, messages and orders.
  • Delivery addresses, for products you order.
  • Orders, wallet and transaction records.
  • If you sell on NAMO: your payout account and PAN, stored encrypted.

We do not store card, UPI or bank details you pay with; Razorpay and PhonePe handle those.

Why we hold it

To run your account and feeds, compute charts, let consultants deliver sessions, take payments, deliver products, pay sellers, keep NAMO safe, and meet legal and tax obligations. We do not sell your data and do not use it for third-party advertising.

Who we share it with

  • Razorpay and PhonePe — to take payments.
  • Daily.co — to carry audio and video calls between you and a consultant.
  • Google Firebase Cloud Messaging — to deliver notifications to your phone.
  • Google Gemini — your question, the chart it is about and the name on that chart, to write NAMO AI's answer; never your phone number.
  • freeastroapi.com — your birth date, time and place, to compute your chart; never your name, phone number or account.
  • Our courier partners (Shiprocket and the couriers it uses) — your name, address and phone, to deliver a product.
  • The consultant you book — your name and the birth details for that booking.
  • A seller whose download, course or event you buy — that you bought it, so they can serve you; never your phone number or address.
  • Our hosting and service providers — Supabase (sign-in), Google Cloud (our servers), Cloudflare (stored files) and Twilio (sign-in codes).
  • Authorities, where the law requires it.

What you post publicly is visible to other people on NAMO.

Retention

We keep account and transaction records while your account is open, and afterwards for as long as tax and accounting law requires.

Your choices

You can view and correct your details in the app, and turn notifications off in your phone's settings. To delete your account, go to Settings → Delete my account in the app, or follow these steps: your profile is erased and you are signed out for good; wallet and transaction records are kept as long as tax and accounting law requires. To get a copy of your data, write to support@1namo.com.

Security

Access to data is controlled so one account cannot read another's. Payment secrets and service keys are never exposed to the app; payout accounts and PAN are encrypted.

Changes

The version on this page is the current one. Material changes will be noted in the app.
