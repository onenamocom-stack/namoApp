APP NAME (30 max)
Namo – Connecting Bharat

SHORT DESCRIPTION (80 max)
Live darshan, status, jaap, kundli, astrologers and a Hindu social network

FULL DESCRIPTION (4000 max)
Namo connects हिन्दु-स्थान. Darshan of India's temples, your daily bhakti, astrology you can act on, and a social network for people who share your faith. One app.

LIVE DARSHAN
Watch the aarti live from temples across India: Jyotirlingas, Shakti Peeths, Char Dham, Krishna, Hanuman, Ganesh and Jain tirths. Temples that stream live come first. When a temple is not live, you see the deity's darshan.

STATUS AND WALLPAPERS
A fresh bhakti status every day: tithi, festival and deity cards, ready to share. Wallpapers of more than a hundred deities, sized for your phone.

JAAP COUNTER
Count your mantra jaap with one tap. Set a goal of 9, 11, 21, 51, 108 or 1008. The phone buzzes on every count and the ghanta rings when your mala is complete. The screen stays on while you chant.

ASTROLOGY
- Daily rashifal and panchang
- Your kundli from your birth date, time and place
- Kundli milan for marriage
- Muhurat for your important days
- Ask NAMO AI about your chart
- Tarot

TALK TO AN ASTROLOGER
Chat, audio call or video call with astrologers approved by Namo. You see the rate per minute before you start, and you pay only for the minutes you talk.

SOCIAL
Post photos, reels and thoughts. Follow people, join groups, message friends, and see bhakti from people who share your faith.

MARKET
Puja samagri and books from sellers on Namo, delivered to your door.

SAFE BY DESIGN
Report or block anyone. Delete your account from Settings whenever you choose.

Namo is for people aged 18 and over. Astrology and tarot readings are guidance, not a promise of any outcome, and are no substitute for medical, legal or financial advice.
